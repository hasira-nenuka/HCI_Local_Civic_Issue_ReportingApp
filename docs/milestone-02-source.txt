1.  Introduction
1.1  Background  of  the  Local  Civic-Issue  Reporting  App
The  Local  Civic-Issue  Reporting  App  is  a  proposed  mobile-based  system  designed  to  provide  a  more  structured
and

convenient

way

for

citizens

to

report

and

monitor

local

civic

issues.

The

system

focuses

on

common

community

problems

such

as

damaged

roads,

garbage-related

issues,

water-supply

problems,

drainage

issues,

broken

streetlights,

and

other

environmental

or

public-service-related

issues.

The  user  research  conducted  during  Assignment  01  identified  several  difficulties  in  the  existing  civic-issue
reporting

process.

From

the

citizen

perspective,

users

may

not

always

know

which

authority

is

responsible

for

a

particular

issue.

They

may

also

experience

difficulties

when

explaining

the

exact

location

of

an

issue

through

telephone-based

reporting.

In

addition,

citizens

may

not

receive

sufficient

information

after

submitting

a

complaint

about

whether

it

has

been

received,

assigned,

or

resolved.

The  research  also  identified  challenges  faced  by  Pradeshiya  Sabha  Officers.  Complaints  may  be  received
through

different

channels

and

may

contain

incomplete

descriptions

or

unclear

location

information.

The

research

indicated

that

complaint

records

are

mainly

maintained

using

paper-based

methods,

while

some

records

are

maintained

using

Excel.

These

practices

can

make

complaint

organization,

monitoring,

prioritization,

and

follow-up

more

difficult.

The  Grama  Niladhari  (GN)  interview  also  highlighted  communication  and  follow-up  difficulties.  Residents  may
approach

the

GN

when

they

are

unsure

where

to

report

an

issue

or

when

they

require

information

about

the

progress

of

an

existing

complaint.

Without

centralized

complaint-status

information,

obtaining

and

communicating

current

progress

can

require

additional

coordination

with

the

relevant

authority.

Based  on  these  research  findings,  the  proposed  system  provides  features  to  support  the  complete  civic-issue
reporting

and

management

process.

These

include

selecting

an

issue

category,

providing

issue

details,

uploading

photographs,

confirming

the

location

using

GPS,

submitting

complaints,

tracking

complaint

progress,

receiving

notifications,

and

managing

complaints

through

an

administrative

interface.

The  design  of  the  system  is  therefore  based  on  the  user  requirements  identified  during  Assignment  01.  The  main
functional

requirements

include

allowing

citizens

to

submit

complaints

with

descriptions,

photographs,

and

GPS

locations;

enabling

citizens

to

track

complaints;

providing

notifications

when

complaint

statuses

change;

allowing

Pradeshiya

Sabha

Officers

to

manage

complaints;

and

supporting

Grama

Niladharis

in

monitoring

complaint

progress

within

their

divisions.

1.2  Purpose  of  Milestone  02
The  purpose  of  Milestone  02  is  to  transform  the  user  requirements  and  research  findings  identified  in
Assignment

01

into

user-centred

interface

designs

and

interactive

prototypes.

The  design  process  follows  a  structured  sequence  beginning  with  the  development  of  initial  sketches  and  design
alternatives.

The

selected

design

concepts

are

then

developed

into

wireframes

to

define

the

layout,

navigation,

content

structure,

and

interaction

elements

of

the

system.

These

wireframes

are

further

developed

into

a

low-fidelity

prototype

to

represent

the

main

interactions

and

user

flows.

The  low-fidelity  prototype  is  evaluated  through  validation  and  refinement  activities.  Based  on  the  identified
usability

issues

and

feedback,

the

designs

are

improved

before

developing

the

high-fidelity

prototype.

The

high-fidelity

prototype

represents

the

proposed

interface

in

greater

detail

and

demonstrates

the

expected

interaction

flow

of

the

final

system.

The  milestone  also  includes  a  user  testing  plan  to  evaluate  whether  the  proposed  interfaces  support  the
identified

user

requirements.

The

testing

focuses

on

aspects

such

as

task

completion,

navigation,

usability,

understanding

of

interface

elements,

and

the

ability

of

users

to

complete

important

activities

within

the

system.

Through  this  process,  Milestone  02  aims  to  demonstrate  how  the  findings  from  the  user  research  have  been
translated

into

practical

interface

design

solutions

for

the

Citizen,

Pradeshiya

Sabha

Officer,

and

administrative

interactions

of

the

Local

Civic-Issue

Reporting

App.


1.3  Scope  of  the  Prototype
T he  prototype  covers  the  main  user  interactions  required  to  support  the  local  civic-issue  reporting  process.  The
system

is

designed

around

the

three

main

stakeholder

groups

identified

during

Assignment

01:

Citizens,

Pradeshiya

Sabha

Officers,

and

Grama

Niladharis/administrative

users
.

Citizen-side  Prototype
The  Citizen-side  prototype  covers  the  complete  reporting  and  complaint-monitoring  journey.  The  main  screens
include:

●  Login  ●  Sign  Up  ●  Home  ●  Report  Issue  –  Category  Selection  ●  Issue  Type  Selection  ●  Area  Selection  ●  Local  Authority  Selection  ●  Complaint  Form  ●  Location  Confirmation  ●  Submission  Confirmation  ●  My  Reports  ●  Complaint  Tracking  ●  Notifications  4

 ●  Notification  Details  ●  Profile  ●  Profile  Editing  ●  Search  and  Filter  Reports  ●  Report  Details  ●  Category  Information
These  screens  support  the  citizen  from  the  initial  login  and  issue  reporting  process  through  to  complaint
tracking,

receiving

status

notifications,

viewing

complaint

details,

and

managing

profile

information.

Officer/Admin-side  Prototype
The  Officer/Admin  prototype  covers  the  main  complaint-management  and  administrative  activities.  The
prototype

includes

screens

such

as:

●  Splash  Screen  ●  Login  /  Sign  Up  ●  Admin  Dashboard  ●  Complaints  List  ●  Complaint  Details  ●  Settings  ●  Analytics  /  Summary  ●  Profile  ●  Complaint  Assignment  ●  Complaint  Progress  ●  Service  Categories  ●  User  Management  ●  Contact  Finder  ●  Pradeshiya  Sabha  Officer  Management  ●  GN  Management

2.1  Functional  Requirements






                      6
ID  Requirement  Research  Evidence
FR1  Allow  citizens  to  submit  civic  issue  reports  with  a  description,  photo,  and  GPS  location.
Most  citizen  respondents  selected  Photo  Upload  and  GPS  Location  as  essential  features  for  accurate  issue  reporting.  FR2  Enable  citizens  to  track  the  status  of  their  complaints  from  submission  to  resolution.
Many  respondents  reported  receiving  no  updates  after  submitting  complaints  and  requested  Complaint  Tracking.  FR3  Provide  automatic  notifications  whenever  the  complaint  status  changes.
Participants  identified  Notifications  as  one  of  the  most  important  application  features.
FR4  Allow  Pradeshiya  Sabha  officers  to  view,  prioritize,  and  manage  complaints  through  a  dashboard.
Government  officers  selected  Complaint  Dashboard,  Complaint  Priority,  and  Status  Management  as  useful  features.  FR5  Allow  Grama  Niladhari  officers  to  monitor  complaint  progress  within  their  division  and  coordinate  with  the  Pradeshiya  Sabha.
This  requirement  is  derived  from  the  inferred  Grama  Niladhari  persona,  supported  by  survey  findings  highlighting  the  need  for  complaint  tracking  and  improved  communication.


2.2  Non-Functional  Requirements



7
ID  Requirement  Research  Evidence
NFR1  Easy-to-use  interface.  Many  respondents  preferred  a  simple  and  user-friendly  mobile  application  for  reporting  civic  issues.  NFR2  Fast  response  time.  Survey  participants  identified  slow  response  as  one  of  the  major  challenges  in  the  current  complaint  handling  process.  NFR3  Secure  user  data.  Citizens  provide  personal  information  and  complaint  details;  therefore,  the  application  should  protect  user  data  and  privacy.  NFR4  High  availability  (24/7).  Civic  issues  can  occur  at  any  time,  so  users  should  be  able  to  submit  complaints  whenever  necessary.  NFR5  Reliable  notification  service.  Many  respondents  selected  Notifications  and  Complaint  Tracking  as  important  features  to  receive  timely  updates  on  complaint  status.

7.   Prototyping  Tool  Justification
Figma  was  selected  as  the  primary  prototyping  tool  for  developing  both  the  low-fidelity  and  high-fidelity
prototypes

of

the

proposed

application.

It

provides

a

user-friendly

and

flexible

environment

for

creating

initial

wireframes

as

well

as

detailed

and

visually

refined

interfaces.

Figma

supports

real-time

collaboration,

allowing

all

four

team

members

to

work

together

on

the

same

prototype

without

version

conflicts.

It

also

enables

the

team

to

gradually

transform

the

low-fidelity

design

into

a

high-fidelity

prototype

by

adding

colours,

typography,

icons,

images,

and

realistic

interface

elements.

In

addition,

Figma

provides

interactive

prototyping

features

such

as

clickable

buttons,

navigation,

transitions,

and

animations,

allowing

user

flows

to

be

demonstrated

without

implementing

the

actual

system.

The

prototype

can

be

easily

shared

through

a

web

link,

making

it

convenient

for

lecturers,

evaluators,

and

test

users

to

access.

Since

Figma

was

already

used

to

create

the

low-fidelity

prototype

in

Section

4,

the

same

design

can

be

further

developed

into

the

high-fidelity

prototype,

ensuring

consistency

and

reducing

unnecessary

rework.
