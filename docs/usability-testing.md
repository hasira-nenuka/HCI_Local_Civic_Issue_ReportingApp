# Five-participant usability evaluation

This is a plan and blank recording template. No participants have been recruited or tested by the agent. Milestone 03 requires at least five real/proxy users on the working app. Do not reuse prototype-test outcomes as implementation-test outcomes.

Recruit three citizens, one Pradeshiya Sabha officer/proxy, and one GN/admin proxy. Explain the purpose, ask consent, use fictional locations/photos and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. Give only the task; do not coach unless the participant is stuck. Record help as assisted completion.
